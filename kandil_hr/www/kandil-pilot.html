Walk through recruitment / خطوات تجربة التوظيف
  1. Request / الطلب: create a hiring request with a location and quantity on every line, save, then Submit for Approval. One request can include several locations.
  2. Approval / الاعتماد: the active department approver, then HR, reviews the request. A justified capacity exception requires the independent Sector authority first. Only the current authorized actor sees the decision buttons.
  3. Candidate / المرشح: use an opening generated by the approved request. Phone-only applicants are supported; record interviews and feedback on their native screens.
  4. Offer / العرض: create and submit an accepted offer. Acceptance alone does not create an Employee or occupied headcount.
  5. Joining / المباشرة: HR uses Record Joining Outcome after actual joining is confirmed. Joined creates an Employee and onboarding task; No Show keeps the vacancy available.
  6. Review / المراجعة: inspect headcount and the audit trail. Cancel unfilled recruitment if required; employees already joined remain recorded.
What still needs HR agreement / ما يحتاج موافقة HR

The full organization hierarchy and CEO rules, offer authority, quantity amendments, shared phones, joining corrections, transfers/exits and detailed privacy scope still need business review. The pilot uses a configured department route. Use separate requester, approver and HR accounts to test authority; Administrator is for exploration, not proof of a business role.

Record-level permissions continue to apply inside each Frappe screen. Shortcuts do not grant extra access. Odoo and other company systems remain separate.
