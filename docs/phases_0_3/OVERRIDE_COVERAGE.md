# P0–P3 privileged actions and override coverage

Source review for **AT-AUD-002-01**, 12 October 2026. This is an inventory of the implemented Kandil parity application, including adjacent joining/ownership controls already connected to recruitment. It is not an assertion that every future HR exception, payroll waiver or administrator operation is implemented or accepted. No canonical acceptance wording/status was changed by this review.

The baseline requires: **“Every allowed override requires an explicit action, authorized role, reason and evidence when required.”** A meaningful approved request, structural change or Board note can supply the reason; a redundant field called `reason` is not required. A normal approval is not itself a bypass. The table therefore distinguishes controlled exceptions/corrections from ordinary lifecycle commands and administrative setup.

## Source reference decisions

* Frozen `src/Requests.gs:447–458`, `boardOnBehalf_`: HR Sector records an external Board decision using an allowed method, a comment of at least three characters identifying who approved/when, and uploaded proof. That comment becomes the stored on-behalf decision comment. Current `on_behalf_note` supplies the same business explanation. **Do not require a second duplicate comment.** `authority.authorize_board` additionally retains the deciding Board member independently of the recording actor.
* Frozen `src/Admin.gs:118–132`, `delegationStop_`: stopping a delegation calls `requireReason_(p)` and writes the new stop reason to the audit and notification. `src/Services.gs:18–21` requires at least three characters. The original creation reason does **not** explain the later revocation.
* Frozen `src/Requests.gs:303–305`: BLOCK denies headcount shortage before examining justification; WARN requires at least ten characters. This ordering is now in `recruitment.headcount_contract.enforce_shortage`.

## Findings and acceptance limits

1. **Delegation revocation was a real source-backed gap.** The fresh reason/server validation and native dialog now pass the delegation 10 suite and English/Arabic browser checks. Date normalization preserves approval metadata in native JSON saves. No backfill invents historic reasons.
2. **No duplicate Board reason is needed.** The required external decision note and proof already provide the business meaning demanded by the frozen reference. The earlier suggestion to require a second comment is withdrawn.
3. **Durable failed-override/shortage-denial audit is still absent.** Controlled rejection and unchanged ledger are different from a retained failed-attempt event. AT-HC-006-01 must retain that gap.
4. **No atomic edit of a pending quantity is established.** The tested Return → revise → resubmit sequence releases then reserves; AT-HC-005-01/related literal atomic clauses cannot be closed with that sequence alone.
5. **AUD-002-01 remains partial.** The inventory exposes concrete implemented controls, but complete cross-endpoint negative testing and universal operational/configuration exception governance are not proved. Do not promote it solely because many normal approval cases pass. AUD-002-02 still has a separate Administrator operational audit/review clause.
6. Future recruitment waivers, probation/transfer overrides, compensation exceptions and legal-policy acceptance are not smuggled into the P0–P3 claim. Their absence here is a scope limit, not proof they are implemented.
