## Deferred source conflicts

Salary authority (exact grade minimum versus older around-minimum wording), optional expected salary, signed-contract/non-blocking joining, insurance dates, privacy deletion and legal templates remain visible in the full baseline but belong to P4 onward. None is implicitly approved by this first-four-phase delivery.

## Review protocol / طريقة المراجعة

Each business owner decision must record decision ID, affected case IDs, chosen rule, approver, date and effective revision. A developer assumption must remain visibly marked until accepted. Test reports must name the configured rule. No environment should silently switch policy because a document was updated.
