## Exact obligation and stopping boundary

**AT-AUD-001-01:** “Decision records actor, acting principal, before/after, exact command and timestamp; ordinary APIs cannot edit/delete it.”

The frozen source is `reference/kandil-recruitment-portal/src/Audit.gs:9–35`: `audit_` records user/on-behalf identity, action, entity, field, old/new values, reason and timestamp; `auditDiff_` emits changed fields. The baseline cites this across source suites. It does not demand that every record use one central envelope, nor that every native read, draft save, print or notification becomes a business approval. A dedicated immutable history can satisfy the required elements if its concrete invocation and tests prove them.

The finite boundary is decisions that implement the selected 40 requirements and their 80 cases, plus supporting state changes which those cases explicitly depend on. New endpoints added to fulfill those cases must join the inventory; future P4–P10 modules do not silently join it. External chain/anchor protection remains **AT-AUD-001-02**, not an additional criterion imposed on this case.

“Exact command” means the reviewed callable arguments or an honestly identified native field delta/system invocation. Never invent a client command ID or raw HTTP body for a native hook. A scheduler with no known human initiator must retain that limitation rather than label Administrator as the original human.

## What must not become an unbounded extra project

* **Future probation, transfer lifecycle, deferred employee documents, performance, contracts and payroll:** P7/P8/later features are not required to close this decision inventory. Existing negative process-isolation and no-payroll cases retain their own explicit limitations.
* **Draft editing and confidential HR notes:** authorization, isolation and history are already separate REQ002/008 requirements. A plain draft save is not an approval decision. Do not add an invented universal exact-HTTP-body requirement or leak HR-only notes into shared envelopes.
* **Account grants, role/authority configuration and Administrator operations:** maintain existing security events and frozen route inputs. Specific exception governance belongs to ACC003/AUD002 and the approved P1 security packages. Do not claim this inventory proves all privileged administration; equally, do not hold every business decision open until arbitrary console/SQL commands can be captured. AUD00202 explicitly keeps Administrator access logging/review as a separate real obligation.
* **Local MIME capture, outbox delivery, document print/view/download:** their immutable capture/receipt, current-access and retry contracts remain important. They are transport/access events, not additional business approvals requiring a second decision envelope. A deliberate manual failed-notification override still needs its existing authorized-reason/security-event proof under AUD002.
* **Denied commands:** durable denial audit is the remaining explicit HC00601 clause. A transaction that correctly rolls back all success records has not recorded a retained failed attempt. Design that boundary separately; do not secretly commit the rejected business transaction.
* **External audit anchor:** remains blocked under AUD00102 pending protected operator/key/anchor design. Local immutability does not establish tamper-evident storage.
