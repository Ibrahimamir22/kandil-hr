# HR pricing comparison — 53 products / مقارنة الأسعار

Research checked 27 September 2026. Public research, not vendor quotations. Covers the 53 named products in this project; it is not an exhaustive worldwide product census.

**اقرأ السعر مع نطاقه:** سعر HRIS لا يساوي سعر الرواتب، ولا سعر EOR، ولا سعر منصة AI. السعر المنشور أو المقترح لعميل صغير ليس عرضًا لكنديل. الأسعار التاريخية والتقديرات موجودة لأن البحث يشمل المصادر العامة خارج مواقع الشركات، لكنها ليست أرقامًا مؤكدة للشراء.

## How to read the numbers

- Baseline is exactly **1,000 employees** for arithmetic only; Kandil has more than 1,000 and its exact count is unknown.
- PEPM = per employee per month. A support agent, HR administrator, employee record and EOR worker are different billable units.
- Keep original currencies; no exchange-rate assumptions have been inserted. Compare only matched modules and billing populations.
- Annual illustrations generally exclude implementation, taxes, integrations, devices, migration, training, support upgrades and AI usage. They are not total cost of ownership or binding offers.
- Public list rates may be discounted or unavailable for Egypt. Historical proposals and secondary estimates are labelled; unresolved units are not silently corrected.
- A zero software licence does not mean zero delivery cost. No source here establishes guaranteed Egyptian payroll compliance or permission to resell the product.

## What cannot yet be priced reliably

For rows marked Q/U, broader public searches still did not yield a defensible amount. For conflict/context rows the number exists but is not enough for a matched 1,000+ employee offer. Vendor contact has not been performed. Do not present these gaps as zero cost or invent an estimate to make the table look complete.

For a comparable quotation, specify actual employees, legal entities/sites, HR administrators, managers, employee self-service access, Arabic/English, Egypt payroll, attendance devices and shift patterns, leave, letters, audit/export/API access, implementation, support hours, hosting, AI permissions and resale rights. Request year 1, renewals and three-year total separately, including minimum term and employee-growth pricing.

## Total cost model

Year 1 = licence/subscription + setup/localization + migration + integrations/devices + training + hosting/AI/communications + support + internal delivery effort + applicable taxes.

Three years = one-time delivery costs + each year's recurring costs + upgrades/change requests + eventual exit/migration costs. Count work only once when comparing managed-service bundles with software-only alternatives.

## Rejected price matches

- MegaHR recruiting-tool rates are not MegaCom MegaHR Enterprise rates.
- A similarly named US bluworks field-service website is not bluworks.io Egypt.
- Free app-store downloads do not establish free enterprise HR licences.
- Prices printed beside an alternative product on a directory page do not belong to the product being reviewed.
- EOR, payroll processing, core HR, recruitment and AI-service prices must not be ranked as interchangeable subscriptions.
