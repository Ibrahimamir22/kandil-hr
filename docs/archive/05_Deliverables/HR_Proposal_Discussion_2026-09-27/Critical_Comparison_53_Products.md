# Critical comparison — 53 HR products / مقارنة نقدية

27 September 2026. Assessment for Kandil: 1,000+ employees, Egypt primary market, HR currently in Google Drive/Sheets, and a proposed Kandil-owned commercial agentic product.

This is a desk-research assessment, not hands-on product testing. Strengths summarize documented positioning/features. Weaknesses identify fit limitations, cost drivers and evidence gaps; an unverified feature is not asserted to be absent. There is no fabricated numerical score. See the separate pricing register for full amounts and commercial evidence quality.

**معيار الاختيار:** هل المنتج ينجز إجراءات كنديل فعلًا؟ هل يدعم الرواتب المصرية والورديات والأجهزة؟ هل البيانات قابلة للتصدير والتكامل؟ وهل يناسب هدف الملكية والبيع؟ عدد الخصائص أو وجود AI في التسويق لا يكفي.

## A fair shortlist for the next stage

- **Local operational demos:** Mawared HR, HITS and bluworks; add ZenHR and Menaitech as regional comparisons. Selection is provisional and depends on actual shifts, payroll rules and attendance devices.
- **Open-source proof of fit:** Frappe HR first, Horilla and OrangeHRM as alternatives. These are candidate foundations, not a claim that Egyptian payroll or production performance is already validated.
- **Agentic/service benchmarks:** Leena AI, Moveworks, ServiceNow and Zendesk Employee Service. These help compare request completion and service operations; most still require HR/payroll underneath.
- **Enterprise reference points:** SAP, Oracle, Workday, UKG and Dayforce. Keep their capabilities and costs in perspective without assuming Kandil needs every module.

Require the same demonstration from finalists: a night shift crossing midnight, missing punches, overtime correction, leave balance adjustment, Arabic bank letter generated with today's issue date, employee who lacks a smartphone, payroll reconciliation and full audit/export. For payroll, reconcile representative historical data and run at least two full parallel cycles before cutover; HR/payroll owners set the acceptance criteria. For an agent, measure completed valid actions, human touch time, corrections and cost, not just conversational fluency.

The buy route can be the best internal operating decision even if Kandil chooses to build a separate reusable service layer. A vendor with an AI feature is not automatically a competitor to the entire Kandil business; it can also be the underlying system or integration partner.
