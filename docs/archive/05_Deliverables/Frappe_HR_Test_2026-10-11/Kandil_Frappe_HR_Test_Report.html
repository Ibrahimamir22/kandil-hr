Frappe HR: actual functional test

We exercised the installed system with synthetic data through normal authenticated HTTP APIs, then inspected desktop and mobile screens in Chrome. These are runtime observations from this installation, not claims that all Kandil requirements are ready.

18Checks passed
3Confirmed fit gaps
1Unresolved failure

Decision supported: The standard HR foundation handles the tested recruitment, leave and attendance operations. Bespoke recruitment controls and concurrent-write reliability still require work before a Kandil pilot can be accepted. This test does not select Frappe as the company-wide backend.

What worked

  • Hiring requisition → approval by a different HR manager → linked opening → Arabic-named applicant → interview and scored feedback → accepted offer → explicit employee conversion → onboarding task.
  • A configured standard approval workflow blocked the request creator from approving their own request. Kandil delegation, CEO routing and emergency substitution were not tested.
  • An ordinary employee submitted a two-day leave request. HR approved it; the balance changed from 10 to 8. An excessive request was rejected.
  • An ordinary employee could read their own employee record, but could not read a colleague's record, recruitment candidate, or colleague's leave balance.
  • Manual attendance saved; a duplicate for the same day was rejected. This was not a BioTime test.
  • A standard offer PDF was generated after fixing container-local asset routing. Arabic typography and employment-document correctness remain unverified.

What needs work

FindingEvidence and implication
Multiple locations per requestThe runtime standard requisition schema has no child table for location lines. Kandil's combined request and per-location quantities need a design and customization.
Candidate without emailCreating a phone-only applicant was rejected because email_id is mandatory. The exact factory-worker application flow needs adaptation.
Pending headcount reservationTwo separate Pending requisitions saved against one planned vacancy. No Kandil reservation ledger is installed. A staffing plan is not proof that pending requests reserve capacity.
Concurrent writes — unresolvedTwo already-authenticated HR managers submitted requisitions simultaneously: one saved and one returned HTTP 500 / QueryDeadlockError (1020), record changed in tabSeries. Earlier attempts also hit database conflicts. Root cause and production impact are not established. Do not treat this installation as ready for 1,000 employees.
Arabic UIThe employee list rendered RTL and preserved the Arabic candidate name. Labels such as HR Setup, Organizational Chart, Employment Type and Branch remained English. Translation quality and terminology need review.
Approval-to-opening gateDuring the first run, an HR manager could create a linked opening while the requisition was still pending. After the request was approved, the link was rechecked. A strict Kandil rule requiring approval before publishing/creating openings has not been configured or proven.

Test conditions and corrections

Frappe 16.51.1; ERPNext 16.50.0; HRMS 16.50.0; empty Kandil custom-app scaffold. Site: kandil-test.localhost, accessible at localhost:8101. Main site remains at port 8100. Separate databases share the local bench and infrastructure.

Synthetic company: Kandil TEST ONLY, Egypt, EGP; two HR managers and two ordinary employee accounts; five employee records after recruitment conversion. Leave policies, compensation values, holidays and identities are dummy test fixtures, not Kandil policy. Email is muted. Setup used Administrator; functional actions used ordinary assigned roles, except metadata and setup checks.

The initial run had 13 passes, two gaps and six failures. HR manager fixtures initially had self-only Employee permissions; correcting those scopes resolved approval, conversion and attendance failures. Onboarding's initial failure depended on the missing employee. Staffing Plan needed an explicit document name. PDF generation needed the container to reach its own public localhost URL. These were test/setup corrections; upstream app source was not edited. Employee restrictions were rechecked afterward.

Concurrency was isolated as T22 after distinguishing it from the headcount fit gap. The current failure is retained. No retries were used to turn that check into a pass. An earlier simultaneous same-account login also returned a SAVEPOINT error; this was not reproduced in a separate login reliability test.

Results capture a staged scenario. In particular, “no employee before conversion” was observed before creating the employee; replaying that assertion after conversion is not meaningful. First-run evidence is preserved alongside the final results. All four app working trees were clean after testing.

Not yet proven

  • 1,000-employee volume, payroll-run performance, realistic simultaneous activity or sustained load.
  • Egypt payroll and insurance calculations, Kandil leave/shift/overtime rules, historic imports and reconciliation.
  • Odoo, BioTime, Google Drive/Sheets, notification delivery or AI integrations.
  • Full role and field-level privacy audit, backup restoration, deployment resilience and scheduler-driven operations.
  • The complete five bespoke journeys: multi-location approval, quantity exceptions/reservations, email-less applicants, delegated authority, and joining/no-show with headcount updates.

  3. Have HR validate those journeys with synthetic examples, then run a realistic volume test and Egypt-specific payroll/policy checks before importing real data.
