What was built

Request with location lines → Reserve quantities → Independent approvals → Opening per location → Candidate → Interview → Offer → Actual joining / No-show
  • Versioned custom DocTypes, action endpoints, role rules, form buttons and a bilingual review entry page in kandil_hr.
  • Atomic capacity reservations, an exception gate, partial filling, rejection and cancellation without losing actual joiners.
  • Phone-only Job Applicants with internal IDs, optional email metadata and an application duplicate constraint. No fabricated email addresses.
  • Dated, revocable delegation with principal/actor attribution and prevention of delegated self-approval.
  • Accepted offers do not count as hires. Actual joining creates an Employee and an onboarding task; repeated joining creates no duplicate. A no-show retains the vacancy.
  • Server controls reject direct status changes, fake audit events, manual pilot openings and employee conversion that bypasses joining.

The concurrency investigation

The local database is MariaDB 11.8.9. Its snapshot-isolation setting was ON with REPEATABLE-READ. Standard Frappe numbering locks conflicted under that setting. MariaDB documents the changed default and the record-changed error behavior in its official system-variable reference.

Controlled probeConcurrencyResult
Original setting52 of 10 writes saved; 8 failed
Snapshot isolation OFF, row locking retained10100 of 100 saved; 100 distinct record IDs; no retries

The compatibility setting is persisted in this local Compose project's MariaDB command. It affects the shared local server used by both local sites; main-site business records were not migrated or populated by this work. No global Docker setting or unrelated project was changed.

A separate custom-pilot deadlock involved locking an empty approval-step range. The pilot now avoids that unnecessary draft lock and has at most three retries of a fully rolled-back transaction for transient database deadlocks. Authorization and validation errors are not retried. No external effects are performed in those transactions.

Volume test: what the numbers mean

1,000 additional synthetic Employee records were created through normal validation. The test site then contained 1,008 employees in total. Ten authenticated sessions performed a short batch of 600 business operations, using 700 HTTP requests: 300 individual reads, 200 paginated list reads, and 100 attendance create-and-submit pairs.

OperationCountFailures95th percentile
attendance write1000130.58 ms
employee list200036.37 ms
employee read300036.34 ms

The batch took 2.52 seconds on this Mac's local Docker development environment. The 95th percentile means 95% of measured operations completed within that time. These figures exclude network travel to a production server, login storms, 1,000 simultaneous users, payroll runs, biometric history, notifications and sustained soak testing. They are evidence for this pilot dataset, not an SLA or a hosting specification.

Where this pilot differs from the full requirements

  • Approval routing: a configurable department route with an independent exception approver. Full organization hierarchy resolution, effective-dated CEO rules and compensation authority are not implemented.
  • Quantities: approved quantity equals requested quantity. Reductions, return-for-amendment versions and changing an approved position need further workflow work.
  • Capacity: the pilot enforces a justification/exception gate; HR must decide whether the first operational phase should use the blueprint's warning mode. Baseline occupied values are setup inputs. Transfers, exits and ongoing reconciliation/imports remain to be designed.
  • Identity: one normalized contact per opening prevents the tested duplicate. Shared phone numbers, alternative country formats, identity correction and reapplication rules need HR review.
  • Security: requester scope, unrelated-reader rejection, selected employee restrictions and action authorization were tested. Recruiter/Sector roles see all pilot records. This is not a full field-level or multi-company privacy audit.
  • Joining: final outcomes and employee assignments are protected. Reversals, rehire, approved transfers and exits require new audited flows.
  • Readiness: no Egypt payroll validation, Odoo/BioTime/Sheets integration, backup restoration, external notification delivery or production deployment.
