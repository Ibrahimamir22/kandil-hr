# Kandil HR software and Results as a Service research

Prepared 27 September 2026. Public-source research; enterprise offers and capabilities need vendor validation.

## The recommendation

For Kandil’s **1,000+ employees**, start by evaluating a proven Egyptian HR/payroll foundation, then build a focused **Results as a Service** offering around work it can complete. Replacing an entire HR system is a much larger commitment than delivering an AI employee-service layer.

The first demonstration shortlist is **HITS / HR Nexus, Menaitech, ZenHR and bluworks**. These are research-based candidates, not tested winners. Compare Egyptian payroll, attendance, employee access, enterprise controls, integrations and total cost before selecting one.

Kandil currently uses **Google Drive/Sheets for HR**, according to Ibrahim on 27 September 2026. He excludes Odoo as the proposed HR solution because of additional user licensing. Odoo remains a comparison benchmark only. No live Kandil employee data or payroll calculations were inspected.

This report profiles **44 products** across Egyptian, regional, international and specialist markets. It is a broad market scan, not an exhaustive census. Public information cannot establish a final enterprise price, implementation quality or payroll accuracy.

## Buying, adapting and building lead to different businesses

| Route | Advantage | Main burden | Kandil position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buy and configure | Existing functionality and vendor maintenance | Licensing, product limits and implementation quality | Strong baseline |
| Vendor core plus your agents | Build around completed work | API dependency and ongoing service operation | Recommended hypothesis to validate |
| Adapt open source | More implementation control | Egypt payroll, hosting, upgrades and licences | Prototype candidate |
| Full proprietary HR SaaS | Product and roadmap control | Country rules, payroll accuracy, security and sustained engineering/support | Only with repeated demand and funding |
| Operate RaaS across systems | Charge for useful accepted work | Exceptions, human-review cost and disputed outcomes | Pilot a narrow service |

**SaaS and RaaS can coexist.** SaaS defines software delivery; Results as a Service defines the commercial promise. A software platform can power RaaS with specialists handling exceptions. A chat interface alone does not make a results business.

For Kandil, rebuilding an entire suite before observing the monthly workflow is not justified by the available evidence. Identify work existing tools handle poorly, prove improvement, then test whether other employers will buy the same service.

Review core and extension licences before resale. Frappe identifies AGPL-3.0; this report does not conclude that a closed-source rebrand is permitted. [Frappe licensing/deployment](https://frappe.io/hr/pricing).

## Start with outcomes you can define and verify

| Service | Agent work | Accepted result | Human authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee request resolution | Authenticate, retrieve permitted records/policy, gather details, complete allowed steps and follow up | Correct completed case, recorded and not reopened within agreed window | HR handles disputes and exceptions |
| Attendance exception preparation | Find missing punches; collect explanation and manager confirmation | Evidence-backed exception resolved before cutoff | Manager approves correction; payroll validates impact |
| Onboarding completion | Collect documents and coordinate tasks/reminders | Checklist completed and accepted | HR approves employment terms and checks |
| HR letter fulfilment | Prepare approved template and route signature | Correct authorised letter delivered | Authorised signatory |
| Payroll readiness | Reconcile inputs, flag changes and assemble approvals | Evidence pack accepted by payroll owner | Payroll calculates, approves and releases payment |

Example: an employee asks in Arabic why their payslip changed. The service verifies identity, retrieves that employee’s approved pay and attendance records, explains the recorded difference, opens a correction request if disputed, follows the approval and confirms closure. Missing or conflicting evidence triggers escalation.

Use AI for interpretation, extraction, explanation and coordination. Use deterministic, versioned rules for pay, entitlements and permissions. Actions need audit logs, duplicate prevention, retries and approval limits. Salary changes, bank release, discipline and hiring/firing decisions need explicit human owners.

Choose portal, app, kiosk or authenticated messaging based on access. Do not assume every worker has email or a private device. Validate Arabic text and voice separately if voice is proposed; protect salary and identity data from unauthenticated access.

## A result needs a quality rule and a price boundary

Define **eligible cases** before billing: supported request, verified identity, sufficient information and no excluded dispute. Count one case once; reminders are not new results. Reopening an incorrect completion should reverse or defer billing. Agree who accepts completion and resolves disagreements.

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Accepted completion rate | Correct accepted completions / eligible cases |
| Completion time | Complete intake to accepted result; median and 90th percentile |
| Human touch time | Staff minutes per accepted case, including review/corrections |
| Reopen/error rate | Error-related reopens / cases marked complete in agreed window |
| Cost per result | Model + hosting + messaging + support + human review / accepted results |
| Payroll readiness | Required inputs/approvals complete by cutoff; not automatic payroll correctness |

Start with **setup fee + minimum monthly service fee + included results + extra accepted-result charges**, and credits for agreed service failures. Pure outcome pricing is difficult before volume and review effort are known.

**Illustrative scenario, not Kandil data:** 1,500 eligible cases/month × 20 minutes = 500 staff hours. At assumed EGP 150/hour, capacity costs EGP 75,000. Reducing human effort to 4 minutes/case leaves 100 hours costing EGP 15,000, releasing 400 hours valued at EGP 60,000. Capacity value is not automatically cash savings.

If monthly delivery costs EGP 15,000 and the fee is EGP 30,000, contribution is EGP 15,000, or 50%, before development, sales and overhead. These are assumptions, not a proposed price or achieved saving. Review burden, incidents and low adoption can eliminate the margin.

Measure actual case volumes, staff costs, current cycle times and escalation effort before quoting. Attrition and performance depend on many outside factors; they are poor initial guaranteed-result promises.

## Require the same evidence from every finalist

Use hard gates before weighted scores. Missing payroll, access-control or integration requirements should not be offset by optional features.

| Proposed weight | Requirement and proof |
|---|---|
| 25% | Egyptian payroll: accepted sample calculations, statutory outputs, dated rules and update owner |
| 20% | Attendance/workforce: actual devices, overnight shifts, overtime, transfers and downtime recovery |
| 15% | Enterprise controls: scoped access, audit, restore tests, SLA and two comparable references |
| 15% | Integration/agents: documented reads/writes, permissions, events, limits, sandbox and failure recovery |
| 15% | Three-year cost: all users, entities, setup, migration, hardware, API, AI, support and renewal |
| 10% | Adoption: Arabic/English tasks, mobile/kiosk/shared-device access and support |

These are proposed weights, not measured scores. Agree priorities with Kandil; score demonstrated items 0–5 with evidence. Mark untested items “not assessed”. Change the attendance weight if the workforce profile does not justify it.

Use anonymised cases: joiner, leaver, retro adjustment, commission, loan repayment, leave correction, missing punch, overnight shift and transfer. Kandil’s payroll specialist supplies expected outputs and applicable current rules. “100% compliant” marketing is not an acceptance test.

Demonstrate restricted branch access, employee document privacy, an approved correction with history, failed integration recovery and complete export. Identify standard, configured, custom and third-party functions.

Request hosting/subprocessor locations, encryption, retention/deletion, incident terms, recovery objectives and model-data retention/training terms. This is a procurement checklist, not a legal certification.

## Move from Sheets in controlled stages

Identify the authoritative employee list and payroll owner. Map which Sheets hold contracts, pay elements, deductions, leave, attendance and corrections; resolve competing versions and write permissions. No sheet contents were accessed here.

Prepare stable employee IDs, required fields, duplicate handling, effective dates, document access and opening balances. Reconcile counts and totals; preserve a read-only history and field provenance.

Pilot a representative cohort including operational exceptions. Propose at least two consecutive parallel payroll cycles, extending if material scenarios remain untested. Reconcile at employee level and obtain signed explanations for differences, not just a matching grand total.

Introduce agents through read-only/shadow mode, then draft-and-approve, then bounded actions. Retain a responsible fallback team. Payroll go-live and agent autonomy are separate decisions.

A credible budget and go-live date need data-quality, policy, device, integration, staffing and commercial discovery. Vendor implementation claims are not Kandil commitments.

## Turn a pilot into a repeatable product

Test one service first: **Arabic employee-request completion**, or **attendance-exception preparation before payroll**. Choose from measured pain and volume. Do not build every HR module simultaneously without evidence of need.

Make policies, roles, approval flows and adapters configurable. Isolate client data and permissions; record every agent read and change. Keep result evidence and acceptance independently of the conversation. A SaaS business also needs onboarding, entitlements, billing, metering, backups, monitoring and support.

Keep the core HR system authoritative. Avoid a second unsynchronised employee/payroll database. If Kandil initially remains on Sheets, a narrow pilot may be possible after access, audit and data-quality checks; consequential writes need controlled workflows.

Agree generic-code ownership, client-specific work, resale rights and data ownership before development. Client funding does not automatically authorise reuse of private employee data or confidential processes.

Expand only after accepted results, sustainable delivery cost, adoption and demand from additional independent customers. Repeatable paid delivery is stronger evidence than an agent demo.

## The next decision is a requirements workshop

Meet HR, payroll, finance and an operational attendance owner. Establish exact headcount/entities, site and staff mix, countries, pay cycles, Sheets, devices, approval rules, request volumes, budget and decision owner.

Prepare one demo pack and request comparable offers from the four first-round candidates. Price actual headcount and a growth scenario, separating employee access and API fees. **No vendors were contacted** during this research.

In parallel, measure one RaaS workflow with anonymised cases: baseline effort, accepted-result definition, required connections, approval owners and pilot stop/go conditions. That supports a credible proposal whether Kandil buys a suite, retains Sheets for a limited pilot or later funds a custom platform.

## Scope and evidence limitations

Research date: **27 September 2026**. Evidence comes from official public pages and documentation, directly retrieved or indexed official excerpts. Profiles link the primary evidence; price and AI sources appear beside their claims. Recommendations are analyst judgement.

Egypt-local describes an Egypt-based/focused offering supported by the reviewed material, not an ownership audit. Regional describes a Middle East-facing offering; domicile is marked unverified where necessary. International products with GCC editions remain international. Specialist products are not full-suite substitutes.

This scan covers 44 selected offerings, not all resellers, local developers or legacy systems. No product demos, customer-reference calls, security audit or payroll validation occurred. No numeric vendor ranking was invented.

Where live and indexed pricing differ, confirmation is required. Missing evidence is not proof a feature is absent. Payroll specialists must validate current rules and outputs.
